Section 1

Core Architecture: 100% Offline-First & Zero Data Collection

HydroGrow ("the Application", package identifier com.h3dapps.hydrogrow) is built from the ground up on a strict Offline-First Privacy Architecture. We believe that your cultivation data, botanical research, and greenhouse schedules belong exclusively to you.

All data generated during your use of the Application - including grow cycles, botanical plant varieties, stages, journal notes, plant photographs, reservoir sensor records (pH, EC, PPM, water levels, water and air temperatures, humidity), nutrient formulations, dosing calculations, and custom user templates - is saved exclusively in a private, sandboxed SQLite database on your local device.

Our Privacy Guarantee

The Application operates zero tracking servers, collects no user activity data or analytics, embeds no advertising SDKs, utilizes no tracking cookies or user identification tags, and never sells, rents, or discloses personal or device information to third parties.

User Support & In-App Feedback Inquiries

If you voluntarily submit a bug report, crash log, or feature request through the in-app feedback modal or via email, your provided contact email address and optional device diagnostic parameters (Android OS version, device model, app version) are used strictly to investigate and resolve your reported issue. Support communications are never shared with third parties or used for marketing.

Section 2

Android Device Permissions & Explicit Purposes

The Application requests only standard Android permissions strictly necessary for user-initiated core features. No permission is ever used for background tracking or advertising:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Requested exclusively when you choose to take a photo of your plants, root systems, or harvest directly in the Journal tab. Captured photos are saved solely within your device's private application storage.
  • Photo Library (android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Requested only when you choose to import an existing photo from your device's photo gallery into a plant log entry.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used exclusively for scheduling local, on-device reminder alerts for upcoming water changes, reservoir top-ups, and solution health warnings. All notifications are created and triggered locally by the Android operating system on your phone; no remote push notification server is involved.
  • Reboot Handling (android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Permits the Application to automatically re-register your scheduled local water change and reservoir check reminders whenever your device restarts.
  • Haptics (android.permission.VIBRATE): Provides subtle tactile vibration feedback for button taps, slider adjustments, and alarm signals.
  • In-App Purchases (com.android.vending.BILLING): Enables processing of HydroGrow PRO lifetime licenses or subscriptions via Google Play In-App Billing.
  • Network Access (android.permission.INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used exclusively for two user-directed operations: (1) Google Play license verification for PRO features, and (2) Optional, user-initiated backup uploads and downloads to your personal Google Drive via direct encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3).
Section 3

Optional Google Drive Cloud Backup & Limited Use Policy

HydroGrow includes an optional, user-initiated cloud backup feature powered by the official Google Drive API. Connecting a Google account is completely optional; all core features of HydroGrow remain fully functional in 100% offline mode.

  • Restricted OAuth Scope: The Application requests strictly the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope.
  • Zero Access to Personal Files: Under this restricted scope, HydroGrow has access ONLY to files created by HydroGrow itself within its dedicated HydroGrow Backups folder. The Application has zero access to your personal files, photos, sheets, or any other folders on your Google Drive.
  • Direct Encrypted Transfer: Encrypted backup packages (.hydro format) are transferred directly between your mobile device and Google's official API servers over secure HTTPS (TLS 1.3). No intermediate developer server, proxy, or relay is involved.
  • Google API Limited Use Disclosure: HydroGrow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revocation & Disconnection: You can disconnect your Google account at any time in the Application's Backup Center. Disconnecting immediately removes all cached OAuth access tokens and credentials from your local device.

Section 6

Data Retention & User Deletion Policy (GDPR / CCPA)

You retain complete ownership and control over your data at all times. Because we store zero data on external servers, you do not need to submit requests to delete your personal profiles from a central database.

You can delete all application data through the following straightforward options:

1
In-App Factory Reset

Open Settings > Factory Reset. This permanently and irrevocably deletes the SQLite database, all plant entries, history charts, and local photos.

2
Google Drive Cleanup

Open your Google Drive app or web interface, locate the HydroGrow Backups folder, and delete the .hydro backup archives.

3
Uninstall Application

Uninstalling HydroGrow from your Android device immediately purges the Application's private sandboxed database and all local cache files.

For full step-by-step guidance on account and data deletion, visit our dedicated Data Deletion Guide.

Abschnitt 3

Optionales Google Drive Backup & Google API-Richtlinien

HydroGrow bietet eine optionale, vom Benutzer initiierte Cloud-Backup-Funktion über die offizielle Google Drive API. Die Verknüpfung eines Google-Kontos ist vollkommen freiwillig; alle Hauptfunktionen von HydroGrow bleiben im 100% Offline-Modus uneingeschränkt nutzbar.

  • Eingeschränkter OAuth-Bereich: Die Anwendung fordert strikt nur den Bereich https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file an.
  • Kein Zugriff auf persönliche Dateien: Unter diesem eingeschränkten Bereich hat HydroGrow NUR Zugriff auf Dateien, die HydroGrow selbst in seinem speziellen Ordner HydroGrow Backups erstellt hat.
